Knocked and answered count enrolled homes (each unit is its own home), or with All addresses every address in the doorknock app, with at least one knock, or at least one conversation or busy answer, since 1 June 2026. Roll totals for suburbs and the district include enrolled homes outside the doorknock maps, and leave out unknocked homes in buildings a volunteer has reported as no access or letterbox only; all-address totals cover the doorknock maps only. Support is supporters (4–5) per answered door, one result per enrolled dwelling, with each volunteer's scoring habits removed so every area is scored as if the district's usual mix of volunteers knocked it. The ± figure is a 95% margin of error. Score counts a supporter as 1, an opposer as 0 and a busy or 3 as 0.25 × the household's lean (support ÷ (support + opposition)) ÷ the district average lean, per answered door and volunteer-adjusted, the same score the projections page ranks on.
